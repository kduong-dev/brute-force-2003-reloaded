# Playable demo (`bf_play`)

*This folder was the page `play.md`, split into one file per section (#393): the sections are listed under **Sections** at the end of this page, and `../play.md` is now a pointer to them.*

| Keyboard / mouse (no gamepad for now) | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | move (relative to the camera); the character turns toward the direction |
| Shift | sprint (the game's dash, at the type's dash speed, 8.5-8.7 m/s; WASD alone runs at its run speed, 7.8-7.9: **Ground speed**, #345). `BF_TEST_SPRINT=<s>` sprints toward the goal from that time with `BF_TEST_GOTO` |
| Ctrl | walk |
| Right mouse (hold) | aim: the gun is held raised (its ready timer at 4 s or more), so the camera moves in as after a shot and eases out the game's way after the release ([camera.md](../camera.md): a keyboard-and-mouse adaptation, the game has no aim without shooting); the gun is turned onto the crosshair while the legs keep moving; moving away from the aim backpedals. Moving across it (30-150 degrees off the crosshair) side-steps, standing (`Sc_w1/w2_rp_f_side_walk_l/r` up to 100 degrees, `rp_b_side_walk_l/r` past it) or crouched (`cr_f/b_side_walk_l/r`). Those clips twist the upper body about a quarter turn off the root, and their root motion runs straight along it, forward or back, as far as the walks. So the character turns until the gun is on the crosshair and walks where the clip takes it: across the aim, a little forward or back. One clip in the squad's data has no root motion: Tex's `Sc_w1_rp_f_side_walk_l` (his first slot, whatever gun is in it, aimed left; its own speed h_f9628571 is 5.9e-05 m/s where the squad's other side steps carry 1.0-2.1). The game doesn't move a walk by its clip (FUN_0012c6c0 moves it at the type's `speeds` along the stick), so there he steps left as he does right. The demo moves by root motion, so a side step whose own speed is under 0.001 (the game's no-speed threshold for the play rate) takes the root motion of its stance's walk in the same direction, forward or back (`root_stand_ins`; #170: he walked in place before). `BF_TEST_STRAFE=<x>[,<y>]` moves that way (camera-relative) with `BF_TEST_GOTO`; with a goal far ahead (`BF_TEST_GOTO=4.5,20,4.5,-80`) the camera keeps still while he steps. `BF_MOVE_LOG=1` prints the controlled character's position, facing, gait and clip every half second (and the stand-in walk, `root from`). Verified (#170, `BF_MOVE_LOG=1 BF_TEST_AIM=1 BF_TEST_GOTO=4.5,20,4.5,-80 -- --test`, Tex with the Therm Sweeper): aimed left (`BF_TEST_STRAFE=-1,0`) 1.59 m/s and right 1.60, back-left / back-right 1.53 / 1.54, crouched 1.72-1.73 each way; the planted boot holds within 1 px of the floor on both steps; main's build stepped left in place; Hawk's aimed steps and Tex's unaimed walk are pixel-identical to main |
| Left mouse (hold) | fire the held weapon (at its own rate; also aims while held). Standing, the feet turn with the aim (the spine keeps at most ~11° of twist, also when looking down and turning). As in the game, the camera moves in along the crosshair's ray from the first shot (the aim shift: Tex's 3.9 m to 2.2), stays in for the ready timer's 4 s and 2.33 s more, then eases out over 0.67 s; a dash, a jump, a crouch's transition, a throw, a reload or a switch lowers the gun and takes it out sooner ([camera.md](../camera.md)) |
| Q | switch between the character's two weapons (animated: stow one, draw the other; plays the game's hard-coded switch sounds ff820fda / e1e97460, 0.65 s apart: the HUD weapon panel's, FUN_000cbec0, played only for a character a player controls, FUN_000bb220, so an AI member's switch, a script's toggle, is silent; the controlled character's is heard in a cutscene too) |
| Space | jump: standing jumps crouch first, running jumps launch straight away and carry the run's momentum (Brutus uses his four-legged jump when sprinting on all fours) |
| C | dodge roll / sidestep to the left or right of the movement. `BF_TEST_DODGE=<s>[,<s>...]` presses it at those times with `BF_TEST_GOTO` (to the right, standing); `BF_TEST_DIVE=<s>` dives toward the goal then, as from a grenade landing behind (a squadmate's dive, play_grenade.rs) |
| Z | crouch / stand. Standing still: kneel (the stance's `stand2crouch`, then `rp_crouch_idle`; `crouch2stand` to get up). Moving: the crouch walk (`Sc_w1/w2_cr_walk`, `cr_back_walk` backing off while aiming; the clips' own root motion and footstep events), straight in from a run. Jump, dodge or sprint stands up. `BF_TEST_CROUCH=<s>` crouches from that time with `BF_TEST_GOTO` (held: asked for again each frame); `BF_TEST_CROUCH_KEY=<s>[,...]` presses Z once at each time, as the key toggles. In Tex's berserk Z does nothing and the press is dropped ([skills.md](../skills/texs-berserker.md)) |
| M | cycle the ground surface (changes footstep / landing / slide sounds) |
| click, then mouse | turn the aim, which the camera's view is (the game's right stick; the pitch held to +-60 degrees; no zoom wheel: each character's boom is the game's). Esc releases the mouse, in cutscenes too: cutscenes.md; a click of either mouse button takes it back (the left one's shot is dropped, the right one aims or scopes at once), and an alt-tab releases it and returns it with the focus ("The mouse across a lost focus", below). See [camera.md](../camera.md) |
| 1-4 | take control of Brutus / Flint / Hawk / Tex (on the test map whoever holds their places, any type, or no one: the squad menu, T, #302, [testtools.md](../testtools/README.md)): their name shows in the middle of the screen in the game's heading font (atlas e0afcd52), with three red chevrons (the game's chevron texture 158f87c1) lighting up on the radar toward their portrait and the HUD open sound; 0.5 s on control passes, the close sound playing, and the name, shown 0.4 s whole, swells to 4x and fades over its last 0.3 s across the cut (the game's label, #88: squad.md), and the character you left drops back into the squad AI with the line of its command mode (Cover Me's follow line, "I'm right behind you", "Following at a distance"...: chatter set f415ab02; Stand Ground's 0e69bfe5, Fire At Will's 15dc4e2d: [squad.md](../squad/README.md), #320; from the shared voice bank `ml-sounds/en/common-en.tgz`, through the chatter gate: 100%, a 30 s replay delay, [chatter.md](../chatter.md); their speech icon shows by their portrait while they talk). Not while dead: the death camera hands over by itself. That's a tap (let go within 0.25 s: the game's d-pad, #319); held 0.25 s the key selects that member for the **Squad Command** menu instead, and with the menu open a tap toggles a member: see the next row |
| 1-4 held, then I L K J B V | the Squad Command menu (the game's d-pad held, #319; [squad.md](../squad/squad-command-menu.md), "The Squad Command menu"): held 0.25 s, 1-4 select a member (the radar's portrait turns orange in its bracket, its blip pale) and the menu opens over the paused game, fading in over 0.3 s, the radar sliding to its middle; with it open 1-4 toggle members. Then the orders (the game's face buttons and Black / White in their places): I Stand Ground (Y), L Fire At Will (B), K Cover Me (A), J Move To (X), B Medkit (Black; its heal isn't done, #329; B held on past the menu's 0.3 s fade-out reaches the item box, which uses the player's own Medkit), V Toggle Ability (White). Esc or Backspace closes it (BACK). While it's open no other key or pad button reaches the game (I or the pad's Back opens no objectives menu, Backspace leaves no map). J brings up the Move To cursor with the game running: it snaps to the level's world point nearest the crosshair's ray as the mouse turns the view; the left button or E sends the member there, the right button or Esc cancels. A pad (not under `BF_BACKGROUND`) has the game's own buttons |
| R | reload early: the game's X tapped (let go before its hold time - holding X is the use, the demo's E - or with nothing to hold it for: the player controller 0x1332a0 calls the reload, FUN_00120d40, at 0x134c9d, which starts it only when the clip isn't full and there's a reserve). R reloads on the press, the game's X on the release. The game also reloads by itself on an empty clip, see **Ammo, reload and the crosshair**. The stance's reload clip (`Sc_w1_reload` / `Sc_w2_reload`; upper body on the move, whole body standing); the clip reads 0 (panel red) until the clip's magazine-in event |
| G | use the item in the item box (the game's LT). A thrown grenade (Frag, Energy, Gas, Light, Sonic): hold to charge (the orange meter right of the crosshair, full in 0.625 s, held 0.5 s after the charge ends then fading out by 1 s; the held gun's crosshair stays as it is), let go to throw: 15 degrees above the camera's view (9 above the crosshair), a Frag at 17.8 m/s for a tap to 25.6 m/s full, the meter's bottom 30% throwing as a tap (#161: a full Frag looking 9 degrees down lands ~25 m out); the count drops at once, and the scope is left. A placed one (Roller, Sentry; m09_b's Thermo, #82): set down at the feet at the press. A Medkit (either): used at the press, "No need to heal" at full health. Nothing selected: "Inventory is empty". A Tab or B press while G is held drops the charge, and G does nothing more until it's let go. See **Grenades** and **The item box and the item list** below. A zone starts with the grenades, medkits and items its squad's placements carry, pooled (see **Items: the squad's starting inventory**); the OrgSen is put on at the press (Tex, Hawk and Flint: its clip, then the thermal view) and taken off at the next (#21, [pickups.md](../pickups/orgsen.md), "OrgSen"), Brutus is told "Cannot use", and the Hat does nothing yet |
| E (hold) | use: a gate's wall panel or a console (#262), from in front of it within 2.5 m of its use point, looking at it: "Hold [X] to activate panel." / "... console." shows (the game's glyph line, X's glyph: E is the demo's X; [hints.md](../hints.md)) and a blue ring marks its button; a console held 0.3 s (a panel 0.5 s), the gate opens and stays open, the console is used once. See "Gates, their wall panels and consoles" in [maps.md](../maps/README.md) |
| Tab | the item box (the game's B button): a tap (let go within 0.25 s) steps to the next item carried, in the store's order (every grenade type, the Sentry and the Roller, the Medkit, the 60-point Medkit, the OrgSen, the Hat), round to the first; held 0.25 s, the item list opens round the box and the game pauses until Tab is let go: W or D (or the wheel up) the next item, S or A (or the wheel down) the previous, a step at once and another every 0.25 s while held; nothing else reaches the character meanwhile (movement, look, fire, G, the 1-4 keys). The item scrolled to stays. With a Medkit selected, G heals ("No need to heal" at full health). See **The item box and the item list** |
| I | in a mission, the MISSION OBJECTIVES menu (the game's BACK; the pad's Back too): the mission's objectives, " (completed)" or " (failed)" after each as its flags say; the game's clock stops under it. I again, Enter or Space (the pad's A or B) close it; while it's up no key or mouse press reaches the game (the closing one included). See [campaign.md](../campaign.md) |
| V | the special skill on / off (the game's White button): Tex's Berserker, Brutus's Vengar, Hawk's Stealth, Flint's Targeting. Stamina (the blue bar) pays for it; on needs 10% of it, at 0 it switches itself off. Tex's Berserker draws his other gun into his left hand and fires both; in it Q and R end it instead of switching or reloading. Brutus's Vengar heals him, cuts the damage he takes, charges enemies down when he sprints into them, and turns the view grey with every living character a heat figure. Flint's Targeting widens the crosshair's lock. Hawk's Stealth fades her out over a second: the enemies lose her, the crosshair can't lock her, a shot or an item costs stamina. See [skills.md](../skills/README.md) |
| B | the game's Black button, its Medkit shortcut: held, the 80-point Medkit is selected in the box and used as G would use it (healed, or "No need to heal" at full health), and let go the item before it comes back; "No Medkits" without one (the 60-point Medkit doesn't count); a grenade's charge is dropped; with Tex's berserk or Flint's targeting on it switches the skill off instead. The tutorial's button gates read it too ("Press [Black] to use Medkit.", [hints.md](../hints.md)). B is the shortcut in a test session too (the test tools' NPC fight toggle is ;, [testtools.md](../testtools/README.md)). See **The item box and the item list** |
| Esc | also the game's START for the tutorial's button gates ("Press [START] for game options."): the demo has no pause menu |
| Enter / Space, Q | on a tutorial panel (BASIC CONTROLS, HEADS-UP DISPLAY ...): Enter or Space (the pad's A) accepts it, Q (the pad's Y) skips the tutorial's later panels; nothing else closes it, and no press reaches the game under it (the game's clock stops). See [hints.md](../hints.md) |
